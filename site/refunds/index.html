The short version

  • Previews are free. If you don’t go ahead, you owe us nothing.
  • Stop before we show you the finished site, and we refund the first half in full.
  • Not happy within 14 days of your first care plan payment? Full refund.
  • Cancel a care plan any time. It ends with the month you’ve paid for.

Previews are free

We design a preview of your website’s home page before you pay anything. If you don’t want to go ahead, you owe us nothing.

Paying for your website

  • The first half of the build price is refundable in full until we deliver the finished website for your approval.
  • Once you’ve approved the finished site and paid the second half, build payments aren’t refundable, because the website is yours to keep.

Care plan guarantee

If you’re not happy within 14 days of your first care plan payment, email us and we’ll refund that payment in full, end the care plan and send you your website’s files.

Cancelling a care plan

You can cancel at any time by emailing hello@biomedrive.com or using the link in your receipt. Your care plan ends at the end of the month you’ve already paid for and you won’t be charged again. After the first 14 days we don’t refund part-months. When it ends, we send you your website’s files.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Razorpay, within 5 to 7 business days of our confirming the refund. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We refund the full amount we charged; we don’t deduct our payment fees.

How to ask

Email hello@biomedrive.com with your business name and the date of the payment. We reply within one business day.

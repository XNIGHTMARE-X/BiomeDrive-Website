How paying works.

  1. Free preview

    We build a preview of your new site first. Look around it, with no obligation.

  2. You approve

    Ask for changes until it feels right. Nothing is charged before you say yes.

  3. Live, then billed

    Your card is charged the day the site goes live, then on the same date each month.

  4. Cancel any time

    Email us and it ends with the month you’ve paid for. Your domain always stays yours.

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask us and we’ll reply within one business day.

What do I own?

Your domain, your text, your logo and your photos are always yours. On a monthly plan the design and code are licensed to you while you’re subscribed, and you can buy the Starter site outright at any time for $499.

What counts as a small edit?

Changing text, photos, prices, opening hours or a phone number. New pages or a redesign are quoted separately before we start.

What happens if a payment fails?

Razorpay tries again and we email you. If it’s still unpaid 14 days later, the site is paused until it’s paid. After 30 days the plan ends. The details are in our terms.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you’re not happy within 14 days of your first monthly payment, we refund it in full. See refunds and cancellation.

How do I pay?

By debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express) through Razorpay, a regulated payment company. We never see your full card number.

Where are you based?

We’re a remote, two-person studio in India working with small businesses in the US, the UK and Europe. You talk to the people who build your site, and we reply within one business day.

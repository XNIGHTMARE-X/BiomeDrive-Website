Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask us and we’ll reply within one business day.

What do I own?

Once the build is paid in full, all of it: the design, the code, your domain, your text, your logo and your photos. We send you the files whenever you ask.

Do I need a care plan?

No. It’s how we keep your site online, secure and up to date for you. If you’d rather host it yourself, we send you the files at launch instead.

What counts as a small edit?

Changing text, photos, prices, opening hours or a phone number. New pages or a redesign are quoted separately before we start.

What happens if a care payment fails?

Razorpay tries again and we email you. If it’s still unpaid 14 days later, the site is paused until it’s paid. After 30 days the care plan ends and we send you your files. The details are in our terms.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you stop before we show you the finished site, we refund the first half in full. And if you’re not happy within 14 days of your first care payment, we refund that too. See refunds and cancellation.

How do I pay?

By debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express) through Razorpay, a regulated payment company. We never see your full card number.

Where are you based?

We’re a remote, two-person studio in India working with small businesses in the US, the UK and Europe. You talk to the people who build your site, and we reply within one business day.

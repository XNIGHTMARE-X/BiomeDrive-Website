The short version

  • We only collect what we need to build your site, bill you and reply to you.
  • Card payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see your full card number.
  • No analytics, no advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • Email hello@biomedrive.com to see, correct or delete what we hold.

Who is responsible

BiomeDrive (OPC) Private Limited, {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}, India, is responsible for your personal data. Contact us at hello@biomedrive.com.

What we collect

  • From clients: name, business name, email, phone, business address, and the content you send for your website.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We see the payment amount, date and the last digits of the card, never the full card number.
  • When you contact us: what you write, and the email address you write from. Our contact form doesn’t send anything itself; it opens your own email.
  • Business listings: public information about businesses (name, address, phone, reviews) from listings such as Google Maps, which we use to prepare previews and to contact businesses.
  • Visitors to this site: our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

Why we use it

  • To build and run your website, bill you and answer you (to carry out our contract with you).
  • To keep financial records (because Indian tax law requires it).
  • To contact businesses about our services (our legitimate interest in finding clients). Every such email tells you how to opt out, and we stop when you ask.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the service:

  • Razorpay, to take payments.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our websites and handles our email routing.
  • Google, for our email (Gmail) and for the fonts on this site (your browser loads them from Google’s servers).

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Where it’s processed

We’re based in India, so your data is processed in India and by the providers above, who may store it in other countries. We only use well-established providers with appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

Client records are kept while you’re a client and then for as long as Indian tax law requires (up to 8 years). Enquiries that don’t become projects are deleted after 12 months. If you ask us not to contact you, we keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list so we can respect that.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@biomedrive.com. We reply within one business day and act within 30 days. If you’re in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores two small things in your own browser: that you’ve seen the opening animation (for this visit only) and, on the pricing page, the currency you picked. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to protect the site from abuse.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here with a new date. If the change matters to existing clients, we’ll email you.

Questions about your data? Contact us.

  • The build price is the one shown on our pricing page, or in your written quote for a bigger project.

5. Care plans

  • A care plan is optional. It covers hosting, your security certificate, backups, keeping your domain connected, and the edits listed for it on our pricing page.
  • Your care plan starts on the day your website goes live (the “go-live date”).
  • You authorise us, through our payment provider Razorpay, to charge your card the care plan price on the go-live date and on the same date every month after that, until you cancel.
  • We’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice by email before any price change. You can cancel before it takes effect.

6. Late or failed payments

If a care plan payment fails, Razorpay tries again and we email you. If it is still unpaid 14 days after the due date, we may pause your website (visitors see a “temporarily unavailable” page) until it is paid. If it is still unpaid 30 days after the due date, we may end your care plan and take the website off our hosting. If your build is paid in full, we’ll send you its files. Your domain name stays yours in every case.

7. Cancelling a care plan

You can cancel a care plan at any time by emailing hello@biomedrive.com or using the link in your receipt. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you’ve already paid for. Refunds are covered in our refunds and cancellation policy.

When your care plan ends, we send you your website’s files so you can host it anywhere, and take it off our hosting at the end of your paid month.

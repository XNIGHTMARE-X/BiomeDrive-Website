The short version

  • Nothing is shipped. Your website is delivered online.
  • A preview of your home page in about three business days.
  • Your finished site goes live within two business days of your approval and final payment.

Everything is digital

All our services are delivered online. There are no physical goods and nothing is shipped. We work with businesses in the US, the UK and Europe from India, by email and video call.

How long things take

Times are in business days (Monday to Friday, India time), counted from when we have what we need from you.

  • Preview of your home page: usually ready within 3 business days.
  • Finished website: within 5 business days of your first payment for a one-page site, or 10 for a business site. Bigger projects get a date in their quote.
  • Going live: within 2 business days of your approval and final payment, once your domain is connected.
  • Edits on one-page care: within 5 business days.
  • Edits on business site care: within 48 hours.
  • Your files: sent within 2 business days of asking, once the build is paid in full.

What “delivered” means

A website is delivered when it is live on your domain. If you host it yourself, it is delivered when we send you the files. We confirm every delivery by email.

What we need from you

Your business details, any photos or logo you’d like used, and access to your domain’s settings (or we can buy and set up a domain in your name). If something we need is late, the times above start once we have it.
